Frequently Asked Questions

If this is your first ever hackathon, look here first!

Who can participate?

Anyone! Whatever subject you study, students above 18* are welcome to join!

*Note: Students must study in the Kent area. For example: CCCU, EKC Group colleges, University of Kent and UCA students are eligible to participate.

What should I bring?

A laptop, chargers, your phone, and anything to keep you comfortable (pillows/blankets) for the overnight stay.

Note: You do NOT have to stay overnight at Verena Holmes.

Click here for the Instagram post!

Is there food available?

Yes! Free lunch and dinner on Day 1, and free breakfast and lunch on Day 2 are provided to keep your brain fueled.

There will also be a tuck shop and vending machines for snacks.

What food is available?

The coffee dock and cafe on the second floor of Verena Holmes will be open till 5pm, both days.

Food such as sandwiches and wraps will be available. Allergy information is displayed in person.

For dinner, pizza will be provided as well as soft drinks!

What are the rules?

You must follow the CCCU and HackathonsUK Code of Conduct.

Also, you are not allowed to use pre existing projects.

The Golden Rule: treat others how you would like to be treated!

If you don't, your project will be disqualified and you will NOT eligible for any prizes.

This is the first ever overnight CCCU event, will I be safe?

We have overnight security, and a designated sleeping area for hackers and jammers.

Volunteers and committee members will also be around overnight. They can be identified by the purple shirts with Hackabury 2026 on them!

Download the SafeZone app! This gives you additional tools and support if you are feeling anxious.
